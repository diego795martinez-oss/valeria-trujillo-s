📖

GRAMMAR CHEAT SHEET

1. PREPOSITION PROHIBITION 🚫

Do NOT use in, on, at directly before yesterday or last.

❌ in last night / on yesterday
✅ last night / yesterday

2. "AGO" WORD ORDER ⏳

Ago ALWAYS comes after the time duration.

❌ ago two hours / before two hours
✅ two hours ago

3. SPECIFIC PAST TIME 🎯

When specifying exact past time (yesterday, in 2018, 5 hours ago), use Simple Past tense, NOT Present Perfect.

❌ I have seen him yesterday.
✅ I saw him yesterday.

4. PREPOSITIONS WITH DATES 📅

Use IN for years/months, ON for days/dates, AT for exact clock times.
